## How to use this list

**P0** blocks public launch, or blocks the named feature from being enabled. **P1** is required acceptance/polish for the shipped product. **P2** can follow launch if the Owner explicitly accepts it. Owners below are recommended responsibilities, not assignments already accepted by other people.

**Build** means missing or mismatched behavior. **Configure** means a service/account/catalog needs setup. **Verify** means code exists but the required evidence is incomplete. **Decide** means the Owner or legal/provider review must resolve a product rule. **Operate** means a support/security process must exist.

Mark an item done only with the release commit/build, environment, date, tester and supporting evidence. Record any excluded feature as a deliberate launch-scope decision; remove its promises and entry points before shipping. Deferring a feature is not completing it. Existing sandbox proof should be retained, not repeated with live funds.

## Evidence and current policy references

Source map for the inspected flows: `mobile/src/app`, `mobile/src/features/{welcome,onboarding,profiles,secondary,home,search-ui,lab-ui,messages-ui,chat,booking,appointments,credits,portfolio,social,stories,rewards}`, `lib/{mobile-billing,lab-stripe,lab-stripe-native,mobile-jobs,account-cleanup,account-purge}`, `lib/admin`, `lib/pinterest`, `lib/calendar`, `app/api`, migrations 001–041 and `.github/workflows`. Older `docs/mobile-beta` reports are historical support, not current proof when contradicted by this audit.

- **Store safety and prizes:** Apple requires UGC safeguards and in-app official contest rules with non-sponsorship disclosure. Review the subscription-linked prize structure before activation; Google restricts real-money participation for real-world prizes and applies specific loyalty exceptions. These rules need a use-case review, not wording changes. [Apple review guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), [Google contests policy](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9877032?hl=en).
- **Alternative digital billing:** Eligible territories do not automatically permit Stripe; enrollments, disclosure/API use, reporting and fees remain requirements. [Apple EU payment options](https://developer.apple.com/support/payment-options-on-the-app-store-in-the-eu/), [Google user choice billing](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13821247), [Stripe mobile digital goods](https://docs.stripe.com/mobile/digital-goods). Recheck the exact regional terms before enabling; this checklist does not certify eligibility in every country.
- **Deletion:** Keep the accessible in-app flow; provide a Google web request resource that does not require reinstalling. Apple sign-in tokens also need appropriate revocation. [Apple account deletion](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-review/guideline-reference/5-1-1-account-deletion), [Google deletion requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111?hl=en).
- **AI:** Verify in-app AI-output reporting and prohibited-content controls. [Google AI-generated content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13985936?hl=en).
- **Pinterest:** Approval, permitted usage, attribution and storage/use restrictions need acceptance for LaQue's actual shared collection. [Developer guidelines](https://policy.pinterest.com/en/developer-guidelines), [access tiers](https://developers.pinterest.com/docs/key-concepts/access-tiers/).
- **Retention:** Corporate-tax records have a specific minimum seven-year requirement following the relevant tax period; that is not a requirement to keep all social/account data seven years. Legal/accounting review must set the actual categories. [UAE FTA record-retention FAQ](https://tax.gov.ae/en/faq.aspx?keyword=How+long+must+I+keep+my+records+for+UAE+CT+purposes%3F).
- **Play distribution:** The closed-test requirement depends on account type and creation date. [Google personal-account testing](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/14151465?hl=en-NG).

This audit changed only local launch documents. It did not change production, permissions, billing gates, subscriptions, prizes or customer data.
